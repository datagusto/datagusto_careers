The Role

Latent Deadline Estimation, Uncertainty Quantification
& Counterfactual Evaluation in Production

You will prototype mathematical models (conformal prediction, quantile regression) to infer unobservable deadlines and quality requirements from agent traces, and design off-policy PoC evaluation frameworks. You will build elite expertise in dealing with observational bias for automated decision systems and publishable research experience in systems for ML. Tap each block below to explore details.

What you will work on

  • 1. Proxy Signal Formulation: Define signals (time until output usage, rework, reruns) and specify telemetry logging requirements.
  • 2. Prototype Range Estimators: Build Python models outputting deadlines and quality as prediction intervals (conformal prediction, quantile regression).
  • 3. Counterfactual Evaluation: Design shadow runs and exploration mechanisms to test if cheaper GPUs meet deadlines.
  • 4. PoC Experiment Plans: Formulate control group structures, required duration, and statistical sample size calculations.

For Engineers

It is the workload side, not the GPU side, that knows which jobs are not urgent.

Our scheduler decides where and when AI agent workloads run on GPUs so that jobs finish on deadline at lowest cost. That only works if we can estimate unobservable deadlines and quality requirements.

The Core Frontier

Work on cutting-edge uncertainty estimation (quantile regression, conformal prediction) and define proxy signals for the quality of agent work, such as reruns, rejections, and rework.

Your Direct Impact

You formulate evaluation plans ("did the cheaper config finish in time?") and build Python prototypes that inform live decisions on enterprise clusters.

Global Demand for GPU Infrastructure & Scheduling Engineers

As enterprise AI shifts from conversational chat to 24/7 background agents, inference compute and GPU bottlenecks have become the #1 blocker. Engineers who understand agentic workloads and cluster scheduling command unparalleled value in the market.

The Inference Compute Shift
Inference compute is growing 10x faster than training compute.

Agentic workflows with multi-turn reasoning and tool calls generate thousands of tokens per task. Managing batching, deadline queues, and mixed-GPU clusters is now the defining infrastructure problem.

Enterprise Multi-Vendor GPU Trend
Enterprises run heterogeneous clusters across NVIDIA and AMD.

With GPU supply constraints, enterprises cannot simply buy more H100s. The ability to schedule workloads across older and newer generations under strict SLA deadlines is the most prized skill in modern AI infrastructure.

The problems you solve at datagusto are open frontiers in distributed systems. The experience you build here provides lifelong leverage across the global AI ecosystem.
