For Engineers

It is the workload side, not the GPU side, that knows which jobs are not urgent.

AI agents generate heavy, continuous workloads where nobody is waiting for immediate tokens. Schedulers that pack non-urgent jobs into idle capacity unlock huge savings for enterprises.

The Core Frontier

Work hands-on with KAI Scheduler, Kueue, llm-d, Dynamo, vLLM, SGLang, and DCGM. Profile across GPU generation × model × load level, and measure deadline adherence and cost reductions in live enterprise PoCs.

Your Direct Impact

You will implement placement logic, integrate metrics instrumentation, and support customer cluster deployments, presenting design decisions directly to the CTO at weekly syncs.

As enterprise AI shifts from conversational chat to 24/7 background agents, inference compute and GPU bottlenecks have become the #1 blocker. Engineers who understand agentic workloads and cluster scheduling command unparalleled value in the market.

Inference compute is growing 10x faster than training compute.

Agentic workflows with multi-turn reasoning and tool calls generate thousands of tokens per task. Managing batching, deadline queues, and mixed-GPU clusters is now the defining infrastructure problem.
