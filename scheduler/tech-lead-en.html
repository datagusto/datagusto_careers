About Product

Heterogeneous GPU Scheduling for Agentic Workloads

datagusto connects workload context to GPU allocation. AI agents run tasks 24/7 without a human waiting in front of a screen. Packing non-urgent jobs into idle time on older-generation GPUs dramatically lowers enterprise compute costs.

Heterogeneous GPU Clusters

Execute agent workloads across mixed generations (H100, A100, L40S) and vendors (NVIDIA, AMD) on deadline and at lowest cost.

Deadline-Aware (Minutes to Hours)

Unlike interactive chatbots requiring immediate response, autonomous agent tasks run 24/7 with flexible deadlines, unlocking massive idle-time packing.

Self-Improving Accuracy Loop

Put deadline and quality estimation models into production, reconciling GPU-side and agent-side telemetry to continuously improve placement decisions.

Existing inference platforms pursue response speeds measured in seconds. We work with deadlines measured in minutes to hours. This role makes those decisions actually run on real customer GPU clusters.

For Engineers

It is the workload side, not the GPU side, that knows which jobs are not urgent.

AI agents generate heavy, continuous background workloads. Rather than provisioning expensive new-generation clusters for every job, datagusto bridges the gap between agent priorities and multi-vendor GPU capacity.

The Core Frontier

Most inference engines treat every token request with equal urgency. In agentic workflows, complex research, code review, and back-office pipelines can tolerate scheduled execution. Knowing the deadline unlocks massive optimization potential across GPU generation, model size, and queue timing.

Your Direct Impact

As the systems lead, you own the placement logic, telemetry instrumentation, and integration into Kubernetes/Slurm clusters in PoC customer environments across Tokyo, Taiwan, Singapore, and the US.

As enterprise AI shifts from conversational chat to 24/7 background agents, inference compute and GPU bottlenecks have become the #1 blocker. Engineers who understand agentic workloads and cluster scheduling command unparalleled value in the market.

Inference compute is growing 10x faster than training compute.

Agentic workflows with multi-turn reasoning and tool calls generate thousands of tokens per task. Managing batching, deadline queues, and mixed-GPU clusters is now the defining infrastructure problem.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your key questions regarding the role, technology, and working arrangement.

Why does this position start part-time before converting to full-time?

+

Starting part-time allows both sides to validate technical fit and team dynamic on live customer GPU clusters before committing full-time. We move to full-time as soon as mutual alignment is confirmed.

How does this differ from building low-level CUDA kernels?

+

This role focuses on the cluster layer that decides which GPU a job runs on and when (orchestration, queuing, SLA estimation, and resource placement across machines), rather than micro-optimizing individual tensor math inside kernels.

What is the travel requirement?

+

Periodic on-site customer visits (typically short trips) in Tokyo, and occasional travel to customer sites in Taiwan, Singapore, or the US for joint cluster validation.
