Paradigm Shift

Built for Humans Who Wait vs. Agents That Never Stop

Why we must rebuild the infrastructure layer from first principles for autonomous agents.

Yesterday's Assumptions (Human-Facing)

Designed for humans waiting in front of screens
Usage
A person sends one request at a time and waits for the answer
Demand
Follows human working hours and is comparatively predictable
Provisioning
Compute provisioned around peak demand
What Breaks
Agent adoption drives demand up faster than cost and supply can follow

Tomorrow's Assumptions (Agent-Native)

Built for 24/7 continuous autonomous agent operations
Usage
Agents run long, multi-step tasks autonomously, around the clock
Demand
Workloads that differ from human use in both volume and character
Provisioning
Needs a foundation rethought from its premises (we share our approach during selection)
Engineering Scope
Greenfield system design, built to a global standard

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your key questions regarding the role, technology, and working arrangement.

Why doesn't this page describe the product in detail?

+

We have just filed a patent on the core technology and are developing in stealth. Once the selection process progresses and an NDA is in place, we walk you through the technical approach and the full product.

Why does this position start part-time before converting to full-time?

+

Starting part-time allows both sides to validate technical fit and team dynamic on live customer GPU clusters before committing full-time. We move to full-time as soon as mutual alignment is confirmed.

How does this differ from building low-level CUDA kernels?

+

This role focuses on the cluster layer (resource management and scheduling across machines), rather than micro-optimizing individual tensor math inside kernels or inference-engine internals.

What is the travel requirement?

+

Periodic on-site customer visits (typically short trips) in Tokyo, and occasional travel to customer sites in Taiwan, Singapore, or the US for joint cluster validation.
