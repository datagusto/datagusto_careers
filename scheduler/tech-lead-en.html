FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your key questions regarding the role, technology, and working arrangement.

Why does this position start part-time before converting to full-time?

+

Starting part-time allows both sides to validate technical fit and team dynamic on live customer GPU clusters before committing full-time. We move to full-time as soon as mutual alignment is confirmed.

How does this differ from building low-level CUDA kernels?

+

This role focuses on the cluster layer that decides which GPU a job runs on and when (orchestration, queuing, SLA estimation, and resource placement across machines), rather than micro-optimizing individual tensor math inside kernels.

What is the travel requirement?

+

Periodic on-site customer visits (typically short trips) in Tokyo, and occasional travel to customer sites in Taiwan, Singapore, or the US for joint cluster validation.
